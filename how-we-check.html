How we check facts

We are students, not doctors. This is how we try to be trustworthy anyway.

Who we are

HealthBridge is run by two teenagers in Singapore. We are not medical professionals, so we do not give medical advice or diagnoses. We explain what published research and trusted health organisations say, in plain language, and we show you where it came from so you can check us.

How a MythBuster is made

  1. We pick one claim that people really say, such as "we only use 10% of our brain".
  2. We read at least two reliable sources and link both on the post. A reliable source is a university, a hospital, a health authority, a peer-reviewed study, or a respected science publication.
  3. We give a verdict: Myth, Fact or Partly true, with a short explanation of what the evidence shows.
  4. The person who made it checks every claim against the sources before it goes out. Anything we could not confirm is marked as not confirmed, or left out.
  5. We explain the hard words in a glossary so nobody is left behind.

Our articles and literature reviews

These are written by a team member in their own words, with the author's name on each one. We link the research we used so you can read it yourself (see our reading list).

Our quizzes

The quiz on this site shows the right answer after each question. Our weekly quiz on Instagram also gives a short reason for the answer, based on the same kind of sources as a MythBuster.

If we get something wrong

Tell us using the contact form. We will check it, fix the post, and say what we changed.

What this site is not

It is for learning, not for treatment. If you are worried about your health, talk to a doctor. The numbers to call in an emergency are in the footer of every page.
