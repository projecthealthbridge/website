Psychology

Understanding human behavior, emotions, and mental health

What is psychology?

Psychology is the scientific study of human behavior and mental processes. It explores how we think, feel, learn, remember, and interact with others. Understanding psychology helps you understand yourself and others better.

Mind vs brain

Your brain is the physical organ (neurons, chemicals). Your mind is what emerges from brain activity—your thoughts, feelings, consciousness, and personality. They work together to create your unique experience.

Nature vs nurture

Psychology explores how genetics (nature) and environment (nurture) shape who you are. Most behaviors result from both: your genes provide potential, but your experiences and learning determine how that potential develops.

Key psychology concepts for beginners

Self-esteem

Your overall evaluation of your own worth. Healthy self-esteem means accepting yourself while recognizing areas for growth. It's not arrogance—it's self-respect and confidence.

Stress and coping

Stress is your body's response to challenges. Some stress is normal and motivating, but chronic stress harms health. Learning healthy coping strategies (exercise, talking, hobbies) is essential.

Mental health vs mental illness

Mental health is a spectrum. Everyone has mental health, just like physical health. Mental illness (depression, anxiety, etc.) is treatable. Seeking help is a sign of strength, not weakness.

Growth mindset

Believing your abilities can be developed through effort and practice. People with growth mindsets embrace challenges, learn from failures, and achieve more than those with fixed mindsets.

Mental health support

If you're struggling with mental health, remember: you're not alone, and help is available. Reach out to a trusted friend, family member, school counselor, or mental health professional. Talking helps.
