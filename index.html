Vital signs

20%of your body's energy goes to your brain, which is only 2% of your body weight.
100,000heartbeats a day, pumping about 2,000 gallons of blood.
37.2 trillioncells in your body, constantly being replaced.
20,000breaths a day, without even thinking about it.
86 billionneurons, talking through electrical and chemical signals.

    Our mission

    HealthBridge is a student-led initiative dedicated to improving health literacy and combating medical misinformation among teenagers.

    We believe that access to accurate health information is a fundamental right. We combine evidence-based medical information with interactive learning tools to help young people make informed decisions about their health.
